The first release of MzPCD: the PCF's idea on a distortion, a pattern-controlled distortion, as a VST3 effect.

- **DRIVE, TONE and FEEDBACK** set where the distortion sits; on every step, one of **32 patterns** - MzPCF's - pushes each of them by its own amount, at 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32, sample-accurately through loops and tempo changes.
- **Feedback like a guitar amp's**: the output comes back round through a speaker's resonance into the input. It sustains, then howls at a pitch TONE sets - and the clipper holds it, so it never runs away.
- **2x oversampled**, so hard drive doesn't alias; the dry side of **MIX** is delayed to line up with the wet, and the latency is reported to the host.
- A display of the pattern, with the step playing lit and a meter of the push.

Known limits:
- **Windows only** for now.
- **31 samples of latency**, from the oversampling. Hosts that compensate for plugin latency line it up.
